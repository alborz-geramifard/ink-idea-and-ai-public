Privacy Policy

Ink Idea Poster · last updated October 31, 2026

Ink Idea Poster ("the app") is a publishing tool operated by Ink Idea & AI ("we"). It posts our own cartoons to our own social media accounts. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by us, to publish to the Ink Idea & AI accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Threads and X. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app handles

  • Access tokens issued by each platform when we authorize the app on our own account. They are stored privately on our own computer and used only to publish our posts and read our own posts' links and status.
  • Our own content: the cartoon images, videos and captions we publish.
  • Basic information about our own account (such as the username and the IDs and links of our posts), used to confirm a post went out and to link to it from our other accounts.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about any other person or account.
  • It does not read other users' posts, comments, messages or profiles.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, advertisers or data brokers.
  • It does not use cookies on this website.

Website visit counts

This website counts visits so we can see which cartoons people look at. For each page view it records the page, the website you came from, your country (from the network, not your exact location), the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and how long the page was on screen. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Your IP address is not stored: it is only mixed with the browser type, the date and a secret value into a one-way code, so a visitor is counted once a day and cannot be recognised on another day. The counts are kept on Cloudflare, seen only by us and never sold or shared.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept only while the app is in use and are deleted when we disconnect it. We can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's settings, which immediately stops it from posting.
